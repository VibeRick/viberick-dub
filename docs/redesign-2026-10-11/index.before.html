      How it works

      1. ListenChirp 3 gives every word a timestamp. Gemini 3.8 Flash listens to the audio and corrects names and jargon; the corrected text is aligned back onto Chirp's timings.Chirp 3 · Gemini
      2. UnderstandGemini writes a dubbing brief: topic, speaker style, and how each brand and technical term must be rendered.Gemini 3.8 Flash
      3. Translate to timeEach line gets a character budget from its time slot and the target language's speaking rate, so the dub lands where the speaker talks.Gemini 3.8 Flash
      4. SpeakGemini TTS generates each translated line with a structured voice and speaking-style instruction. Approved creator voices are used only after consent and speaker checks.Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS
      5. Listen back & repairThe agent measures every clip, shortens lines that run long, then Gemini listens to each one. Wrong words or language → re-synthesize, then re-translate, then flag for a human.Gemini agent loop
      6. Mix & shipClips are placed on the original timeline over the separated music bed, loudness-normalized, and published with subtitles.Cloud Run Jobs · Firestore · Cloud Storage · Firebase
