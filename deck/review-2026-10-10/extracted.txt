Brief about the idea
Dub your video. Review every line.
Paste a YouTube link. An AI agent built on Gemini transcribes it,
translates every sentence to fit its timing, uses a consented
clone or a preset voice, then listens back to each line and
attempts to repair what doesn't sound right.
Review a translated line, hear a new take, and accept it without
restarting the whole video. The revised local studio exposes the
actual audio timeline and keeps original captions separate.
Languages today: Korean · English · Japanese · Chinese (source detected
automatically).
Opportunities
A creator needs more than a translated voice
A line can be correct but still sound rushed, leave an awkward pause, or cover the next speaker. Burned-in
captions remain in the source video and can compete with the new subtitles.

The product makes the repair visible
Listen to a language, inspect its timing and review a flagged line. Edit the wording, generate a preview, and
accept or discard it. Other completed languages remain available.

A focused first audience
Tutorial creators and interview producers who want to review a small number of target languages before
publishing dubbed videos and audio tracks.
Studio revisions are implemented locally; deployed validation and fresh listening review are pending.

Additional details / future development
Implemented locally; release verification in progress
Output-aligned subtitles, per-language timelines and translation previews with accept/discard. Timing and
voice prompts are being tested against the recorded baseline.

Next evidence gates
Native listening review for meaning, pacing and voice consistency; measured silence and cost; deployed
recovery and preview acceptance tests.

Expansion after those gates
Validate additional languages one at a time. Add guest consent enrollment and creator glossaries. Export
files for manual publishing; automatic YouTube audio-track publishing is unverified.
Source captions remain in the pixels. Use a clean original for the main demo; preview controls cannot erase them.
